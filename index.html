About

Add photo.jpg to this folder
Instagram @yourhandle

I’m Dimitrii — an editor and content designer working across editorial, graphic and web design. I treat every project as an editorial problem first: what has to be said, in what order, and why it matters.

Six years across B2B e-commerce, EdTech and manufacturing. I’ve owned product content for two B2B brands, rebuilt an e-commerce catalogue around how buyers actually search, and built a structured product knowledge base out of dense technical documentation.

Before that, educational content at scale — a reusable course template system used across 50+ courses, assessment design for two university master’s programmes, and content structure for more than 100,000 learners.

Alongside that runs the print work: books, zines and catalogues for independent publishers. It’s where I learned typography and systems thinking, and it still shapes how I approach a screen.
