About

Porto, Portugal · WET

Replace with your photo

I’m Dimitri — an editor and content designer working across graphic and web design. I treat every project as an editorial problem first: what has to be said, in what order, and why it matters.

My work is informed by UX principles, information architecture and product thinking, which helps me shape intuitive digital experiences that balance user needs with business goals. Typography and layout are how I make that structure visible.

I enjoy simplifying complexity — taking material that is dense, scattered or overwritten and making it easy to understand, navigate and act upon.
