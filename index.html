About

Instagram @cest_horrifique

I’m Dmitrii, an editor and content designer. I work with words, structure, and interfaces to help products explain themselves.

Over the past six years, I’ve worked across communications, EdTech, marketing, independent publishing, and cultural projects, helping turn complex information into clear, engaging experiences.

I’m especially interested in projects related to cinema, art, music, publishing, museums, non-profits, and education.

I design for both screen and print — from interfaces and web visuals to books, magazines, and film catalogues.

Practice

  1. Structure people can navigate

    • Content reviews & mentoring
