UX Case · 2025

Chistetika Lab

A landing page for a product category the brand had never sold

Role
Lead editor & UX writer
Team
Creative director, visual designer, art director, technologists
Year
2025
Live
chistetika.ru/lab
01

The brief

“We sell fabrics and we know how to care for them.”

Chistetika.ru sold interior textiles.

02

The problem

  1. No authority in the category. Trusted on textiles, unknown in cleaning chemistry.
  2. The formula wasn’t ours alone. Composition could not win the choice.
  3. It only works if used correctly. Instructions were complaint prevention.
03

Research

Six direct competitors, read for tone of voice.

A short survey said three things.

04

Structure

The brand block sits above the product.

There is no cart.

Layout: one idea per band.

05

Decisions

Brand values → product benefits

The first benefits row was brand values in disguise.

The most important change in the project.

Showing the composition, not claiming transparency

Every ingredient in cleaning chemistry looks alarming.

This was the answer to formula parity.

06

Writing inside the constraint

The claim I wanted was “safe for children.”

It became “safe for the whole family.”

07

The argument I lost

Two tabs sell products that don’t exist.

Containment limited the damage and still carried a bad decision into production.

08

Result

Live, selling well, strong marketplace reviews.

Structure, sequence and copy went through unchanged.
